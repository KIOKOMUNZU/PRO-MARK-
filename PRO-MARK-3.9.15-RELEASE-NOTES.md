# PRO-MARK 3.9.15 — A4 document and analytics stabilization

- Report cards: corrected header vertical spacing, stronger document border treatment, larger centered school logo, address/contact above logo, school-specific footer email, PRO-MARK watermark and star border ornaments.
- Report cards: term assessment columns are now driven by the actual configured Opener/Mid Term/End Term assessments. Term 3 may therefore show 2 or 3 exams without silently dropping Mid Term.
- Merit: Term 3 no longer suppresses Mid Term when the school has configured/entered it; school contact email is used in document footers.
- Class lists: learner display names sort A–Z by first/middle/last name consistently in the screen and PDF; school-specific email is used in the PDF footer.
- Analytics: moved into a dedicated Performance Analytics tab with subject/class bars, assessment performance, term-over-term deviation, best learners across classes, teacher-to-subject mapping, grade distribution and KPI summaries.
- Mark-entry PDF footer now uses the school's registered contact email.
- No A3 paper requirement.
- No database data is deleted or replaced by this release.
